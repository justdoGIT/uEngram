### Where uEngram wins

- **Rare-event retention under a hard budget.** On the synthetic stream with the DSP encoder uEngram keeps every anomaly event (`event_recall` 1, `seizure_recall` 1, `type_recall` 1) in about 31 items. FIFO keeps 0.011 of events and no seizure; reservoir keeps 0.022 and no seizure (both hold 256 items). With the CNN encoder `seizure_recall` is still 1 but `event_recall` falls to 0.314 (vs naive 1, Reference label-free 0.465).
- **Footprint.** 30.6 items (DSP) and 70.6 items (CNN) at end of stream against a budget of 256, with `type_recall` 1.
- **Budget sweep.** uEngram uses about 32 items regardless of budget; at B=128 and above its `event_recall` is 1. At B=64 it is 0.972 and `seizure_recall` 0.667. FIFO and reservoir stay at `event_recall` <= 0.047 even at B=512.
- **Distribution fidelity (current window).** `mmd2_current` for uEngram is lower than FIFO and reservoir with both encoders (DSP 0.0074 vs 0.0099 and 0.0145; CNN 0.0064 vs 0.0402 and 0.0146). In the paired tables the CNN differences exclude 0.
- **CMAPSS detection and end-of-life retention.** uEngram AUROC 0.749 (DSP) and 0.745 (CNN) are higher than naive kNN (0.673, 0.623) and `eol_retention` is 1 with 4 and 3.2 items, as for naive. FIFO retains end-of-life windows for 0.04 of the stream units and reservoir for 0.432.
- **The doc's engine without labels fails here.** `reference_labelfree` AUROC is 0.139 (DSP) and 0.463 (CNN), below chance on DSP. Its write count is 39,418 on a 20,000-window stream (DSP), i.e. about 2 per window, consistent with a new slot per window plus an eviction tombstone for each. [INFERENCE] The 0.90 cosine merge threshold is almost never reached between noisy whitened windows, so nearly every window is novel to it. The oracle variant, with ground-truth severity, still has AUROC 0.019 and F1 0.045 on DSP.

### Where uEngram loses

- **Detection score quality.** The prequential novelty score (min Mahalanobis distance to a slot) is much worse than point-store distances on DSP: AUROC 0.636 vs naive 0.937, FIFO 0.953, reservoir 0.984; AP 0.0995 vs 0.428, 0.665, 0.845. With CNN the gap is smaller (0.924 vs naive 0.988, reservoir 0.992; AP 0.537 vs 0.838, 0.863). Disabling learned slot variances raises AUROC on DSP: `uengram_fixedvar` 0.709 vs 0.636 (but with more false flags, 0.184 vs 0.062 in era 1).
- **False alarms.** Flag-based detection has F1 0.344 (DSP) and 0.219 (CNN). False-flag rate on nominal windows is 0.112 in era 0 and 0.062 in era 1 (DSP), 0.118 and 0.120 (CNN). Diagnostic for seed 0, DSP (ad hoc script, not stored in `results/`): the flag rate is 0.31 on nominal windows within 4 steps of a regime change (11% of nominal windows) and 0.013 on the rest, and 75% of nominal flags are in the near-change group. [INFERENCE] These are the coolant-temperature transition windows, which are legitimately unlike any stable state but are labelled nominal.
- **Writes.** Reduction vs one write per window is 82.9% (DSP) and 69.3% (CNN), below the expected >90%. Reservoir reaches 93.2% with no detection logic. Promotion matters: `uengram_nopromo` writes 21,916 (reduction -9.6%) because every absorbed window of an episodic slot rewrites its raw window.
- **Retrieval of the current regime and era.** `regime_acc_at1` is 0.237 (DSP) against FIFO 0.908 and Reference 0.95. With CNN it is at least 0.99 for every method except `uengram_nopromo` and `uengram_frozen` (0.967). `type_prec_at1` is 0.98 (DSP) but 0.787 (CNN), against naive 0.977 and 0.951.
- **`stale_at1`, the supersession claim, is not demonstrated.** FIFO scores 0 trivially because it holds only the last 256 windows, all from era 1; Reference also scores 0. Diagnostic for seed 0, DSP: at end of stream uEngram holds 2 semantic slots, one covering regime 0 (3,233 era-0 and 1,765 era-1 windows) and one covering regimes 1 and 2 (about 8,000 era-0 and 5,800 era-1 windows). Neither was superseded (no kind-4 slot exists) because both kept absorbing until the end. [INFERENCE] The +0.5 psi maintenance step plus the wear drop fell inside the gates of these broad slots, so they tracked the change through the `beta_min` step rather than being replaced; the `stale_at1` metric labels a slot by its majority history, so a slot that tracks correctly is scored as stale. The metric and the gate width both need rethinking before this is a fair test of supersession. With the CNN encoder `stale_at1` is 0 for uEngram, but also 0 for every baseline.
- **CMAPSS RUL regression is degenerate.** uEngram `rul_rmse` is 42.7 with every seed and both encoders. That equals the RMSE of predicting the constant stream-mean RUL (85.5; computed separately: 42.74). It stores only 2 to 5 items, `recall(k=5)` returns all of them, and the mass-weighted RUL is the global mean. Naive (31.5 DSP, 27.7 CNN), reservoir (34.6, 34.2), FIFO (37.9, 34.1) and Reference (30.6, 31.7) are all better. The memory is too coarse for this task at the default gate.
- **No gain from the frozen variant on CMAPSS.** `uengram_frozen` is within noise of `uengram` on RUL and AUROC (0.747 vs 0.749 DSP), and differs only on `mmd2_current` (0.0024 vs 0.0291 DSP).

### Caveats

- On CMAPSS the DSP encoder and the stream are deterministic, so seed only changes the reservoir; confidence intervals for the other DSP rows are degenerate (single value), not evidence of precision.
- Synthetic CIs are over 10 seeds (5 in the sweep); `stale_at1` intervals are wide (uEngram DSP [0.373, 0.833]).
- `reference_oracle` uses ground-truth labels and is not a deployable configuration.
- Engine design deviations from the plan (semantic-slot gate priority, predictive gate variance) are described in `docs/EXPLAINED.md`; the plan's engine tests failed without them.
